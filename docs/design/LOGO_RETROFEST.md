## Prompt utilizado
Edit the supplied event poster by extracting and faithfully adapting ONLY its top event wordmark: exact text 'RETROFEST 1.0' on the first line and 'A LA CALLE' on the second line. Preserve the distinctive bold slanted display lettering and overall title identity from the reference. Remove every other poster element: sound systems, people, cobra, side ribbons, corner slogans, backgrounds and extra text. Produce an isolated wide compact event logo on a pure white background, tightly framed but with clean white margins. CRITICAL output for a 203 dpi monochrome thermal ticket: use only solid pure black ink and pure white negative space, bold clean edges, sufficiently thick strokes and open counters, no gray, no color, no halftone, no shadows or gradients. Main RETROFEST 1.0 letters should read strongly in black, with the second line A LA CALLE also clearly readable. This is an adaptation of the attached existing wordmark, not a new unrelated logo. Wide horizontal composition, aspect ratio approximately 2.3:1.
